1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & WHAT THIS SYSTEM DOES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mobile MOBA esports (such as Mobile Legends: Bang Bang and Honor of Kings) suffer
from a lack of open, real-time in-game telemetry APIs. Independent tournament
operators and streamers typically have to use static graphics or complex cloud
tools.

MARK47 solves this by providing a complete, local-first esports broadcast suite:
  * Real-Time Operator Control Desk: Single-click controls for picks, bans,
    timers, kills, towers, and neutral objectives.
  * Sub-10ms Zero-Latency WebSocket Sync: Every button click immediately
    updates broadcast overlays running at 60 FPS in OBS Studio or vMix.
  * Official Tournament Rule Sets: Full MLBB 10-Ban Split and HOK KPL 4-Ban rules
    with automated duplicate hero locks and timer handoffs.
  * 1-Click Event Undo: Atomic rollback that undoes accidental clicks without
    ruining live scoreboard statistics.
  * 100% Offline Capability: Operates entirely on localhost without external cloud
    server dependencies.

3. OBS STUDIO & LDPLAYER EMULATOR SETUP (STEP-BY-STEP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Follow these exact steps to broadcast your game with professional overlays:

STEP 1: SET OBS RESOLUTION (ONE-TIME SETUP)
  1. Open OBS Studio.
  2. Go to Settings (bottom-right) -> Video.
  3. Base (Canvas) Resolution:   1920x1080
  4. Output (Scaled) Resolution: 1920x1080
  5. Common FPS:                 60
  6. Click Apply -> OK.

STEP 2: CREATE TWO BROADCAST SCENES
  Under the "Scenes" panel (bottom-left), create two scenes:
  Scene 1: "In-Game Match"   (For live gameplay + scoreboard HUD)
  Scene 2: "Draft Phase"     (For full-screen hero pick & ban)

STEP 3: CONFIGURE SCENE 1 ("In-Game Match")
  In Scene 1, add these 3 sources in this EXACT order:

  Source 1 (Top Layer): MARK47 Kill Feed
    - Add Source -> Browser
    - URL:    http://localhost:5173/overlay/feed
    - Width:  1920 | Height: 1080 | FPS: 60
    - Uncheck: "Shutdown source when not visible"
    - Click OK.

  Source 2 (Middle Layer): MARK47 HUD
    - Add Source -> Browser
    - URL:    http://localhost:5173/overlay/hud
    - Width:  1920 | Height: 1080 | FPS: 60
    - Uncheck: "Shutdown source when not visible"
    - Click OK.

  Source 3 (Bottom Layer): LDPlayer Game
    - Add Source -> Window Capture (or Game Capture)
    - Window: Select "[dnplayer.exe] LDPlayer"
    - Capture Method: "Windows 10 (1903 and up)"
    - Click OK.
    - Press Ctrl + F to fit the emulator game to the screen.

  IMPORTANT LAYER ORDER CHECKLIST:
  [Top]    MARK47 Kill Feed  (Top-right popup banners)
  [Middle] MARK47 HUD        (Top scoreboard bar)
  [Bottom] LDPlayer Game     (Your actual gameplay)

STEP 4: CONFIGURE SCENE 2 ("Draft Phase")
  Select Scene 2 in OBS, then add 1 source:
    - Add Source -> Browser
    - Name:   MARK47 Draft Stage
    - URL:    http://localhost:5173/overlay/draft
    - Width:  1920 | Height: 1080 | FPS: 60
    - Uncheck: "Shutdown source when not visible"
    - Click OK.

5. TOURNAMENT DRAFT PHASE GUIDE (10-BAN SPLIT & AUTO-TIMER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Draft Room implements official MPL / M-Series competitive timing:

URL: http://localhost:5173/draft

AUTOMATIC TIMING RULE:
  * When you click "START" on Phase 1 (Blue Ban 1), the 40-second clock begins.
  * As soon as a team locks in a ban or pick, the turn automatically advances
    to the next team, resets to 40 seconds, and immediately starts counting down!
  * You do not need to manually click "Start" between turns.

OFFICIAL 4-WAVE DRAFT SEQUENCE (20 TURNS TOTAL):
  Wave 1 - Initial 6 Bans (Phases 1 to 6):
    Turn 1: Blue Ban 1 -> Click hero -> Click CONFIRM BAN (Red button)
    Turn 2: Red Ban 1  -> Click hero -> Click CONFIRM BAN
    Turn 3: Blue Ban 2 -> Click hero -> Click CONFIRM BAN
    Turn 4: Red Ban 2  -> Click hero -> Click CONFIRM BAN
    Turn 5: Blue Ban 3 -> Click hero -> Click CONFIRM BAN
    Turn 6: Red Ban 3  -> Click hero -> Click CONFIRM BAN

  Wave 2 - Core 6 Picks (Phases 7 to 12):
    Turn 7: Blue Pick 1  -> Button turns BLUE (CONFIRM PICK)
    Turn 8: Red Pick 1   -> Hero splash art lights up in slot
    Turn 9: Red Pick 2
    Turn 10: Blue Pick 2
    Turn 11: Blue Pick 3
    Turn 12: Red Pick 3

  Wave 3 - Secondary 4 Bans (Phases 13 to 16):
    Turn 13: Red Ban 4   -> Turn 14: Blue Ban 4
    Turn 15: Red Ban 5   -> Turn 16: Blue Ban 5

  Wave 4 - Final 4 Picks (Phases 17 to 20):
    Turn 17: Red Pick 4  -> Turn 18: Blue Pick 4
    Turn 19: Blue Pick 5 -> Turn 20: Red Pick 5 (Final Roster Locked)

SAFETY FEATURES:
  * Duplicate Lock: Heroes already picked or banned are grayed out and stamped.
  * Undo Pick/Ban: Made a mistake? Click "UNDO PICK/BAN" to rewind the turn
    and clear the hero from the slot.
  * Technical Pause: Click "PAUSE" to freeze the timer if a player disconnects.


6. IN-GAME LIVE MATCH OPERATIONS & 1-CLICK UNDO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
URL: http://localhost:5173/live

DURING GAMEPLAY:
  1. Match Timer: When minions spawn at 00:00, click the green Play (▶) button.
     The clock starts ticking on the OBS Scoreboard HUD.
  2. Logging Kills:
     - Click any player slot or the blue "+ Kill" button.
     - The kill score increments immediately on the HUD.
     - The Kill Feed overlay in OBS shows an animated banner for 7 seconds.
  3. Multikills:
     - Select "Double", "Triple", "Maniac", or "Savage" before clicking the killer.
  4. Turrets:
     - Click "+ Tower" under Blue or Red when a turret falls.
  5. Boss Objectives (Turtle / Lord):
     - Click the "Turtle" or "Lord" button when slain.
     - An objective badge permanently stamps onto the team's HUD wing.
  6. 1-Click Undo (The Safety Net):
     - Misclicked during a crazy 5v5 teamfight?
     - Click "Undo Last Event" (curved arrow button).
     - The kill, tower, or boss count instantly rolls back on stream.

ENDING THE MATCH:
  1. When the base crystal explodes, pause the match clock.
  2. Click "End Game" -> Select winner (Blue or Red).
  3. Winner gets +1 point on the overall series score (e.g. 1 - 0).
  4. In OBS, switch to the "MVP Spotlight" scene to show the match winner.
  5. Click "Next Game" on the web desk to prepare Game 2 (resets clock, kills,
     and towers to 0, while keeping the series score saved!).

10. TROUBLESHOOTING & EMERGENCY CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: My overlay in OBS is black or not showing.
A: 1. Click the "Refresh" button below the source preview in OBS (or right-click
      the source -> "Refresh cache of current page").
   2. In Sources, make sure the browser overlay is placed ABOVE the LDPlayer game.
   3. Check that the source width is 1920 and height is 1080.

Q: Emergency Blackout (Need to hide all graphics immediately).
A: Go to http://localhost:5173 (Command Center) -> click "EMERGENCY BLACKOUT".
   All stream graphics will vanish from OBS instantly.

Q: How do I control the stream from my smartphone?
A: Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi as your PC and open:
   http://192.168.1.12:5173
   You can tap buttons on your phone while playing full-screen on your PC!
